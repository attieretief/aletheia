<div class="lang-af" markdown="1">

*Emet* is die Hebreeuse woord wat in die Ou Testament met "waarheid" of "trou" vertaal word. Dit kom van 'n stam wat "vas, stewig en betroubaar wees" beteken; *amen* kom van dieselfde stam. God beskryf Homself in Eksodus 34:6 as ryk aan liefde en *emet*: Hy is die Een wat sy woord hou.

</div>

<div class="lang-en" markdown="1">

*Emet* is the Hebrew word the Old Testament translates as "truth" or "faithfulness". It comes from a root meaning to be firm, solid and reliable; *amen* comes from the same root. In Exodus 34:6 God describes himself as abounding in steadfast love and *emet*: he is the One who keeps his word.

</div>
