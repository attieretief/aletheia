<div class="lang-af" markdown="1">

Algemene genade is God se goedheid wat nie net vir gelowiges is nie, maar vir alle mense: Hy hou die wêreld in stand, beteuel die kwaad, en laat ook ongelowiges dikwels die waarheid raaksien. Calvyn (*Institusie* II.2.15) en later Abraham Kuyper en Herman Bavinck het hierdie leer uitgewerk. Daarom kan Christene sê: alle waarheid is God se waarheid.

</div>

<div class="lang-en" markdown="1">

Common grace is God's goodness that is not only for believers but for all people: he sustains the world, restrains evil, and often lets unbelievers see the truth as well. Calvin (*Institutes* II.2.15) and later Abraham Kuyper and Herman Bavinck worked out this doctrine. That is why Christians can say: all truth is God's truth.

</div>
